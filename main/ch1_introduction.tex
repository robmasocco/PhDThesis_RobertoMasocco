Pervasiveness. A word that, as its meaning almost suggests, is gradually making its way into everyday narratives, effectively describing the impact of new technologies on our daily lives. It is evident how many tasks and challenges we now rely on, in whole or part, to machines: a growing number that keeps increasing. As we become able to accomplish new tasks and solve more problems with the help of automated devices, new scenarios and opportunities emerge. Research, medicine, logistics, and entertainment: these are just a few of the countless fields in which the introduction of digital and autonomous devices—ever more sophisticated over the decades—has led to innovations at an unprecedented pace. However, this ever-growing diffusion comes with an increase in complexity, both in design and implementation, something that is often praised but more quietly acknowledged as a source of new challenges and difficulties. This is, above all, due to the multitude and variety of tasks that these devices should now perform.
